About the data

Limitations

Important: complaint volume does not necessarily reflect actual rat population sizes. It also reflects how easy a city makes it to file a 311 complaint, public awareness, and how each city classifies rodent cases.

Some records lack coordinates in the source data and cannot be mapped; they are counted per city above (pulled vs mapped vs missing) and are included in the downloadable full CSVs. A few cities publish no coordinates at all — their records are CSV-only. Coordinate precision varies by city (some portals round coordinates). Categories differ between cities; the original city classification is always preserved next to the standardized one.
