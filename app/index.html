Counting mode

Some cities log their own proactive work as 311 records. Baltimore is the big case: most of its records are the Rat Rubout crews' proactive baiting & inspection rounds, not resident complaints. Turn this on to count and map only resident-filed cases. For Baltimore that means the SW-Rat Rubout complaint type, its follow-ups, and HCD-Rodents housing complaints. Cities whose source data has no proactive/resident split are unchanged — their 311 categories are resident-filed requests.

About the data

Limitations

Important: complaint volume does not necessarily reflect actual rat population sizes. It also reflects how easy a city makes it to file a 311 complaint, public awareness, and how each city classifies rodent cases.

Some records lack coordinates in the source data and cannot be mapped; they are counted per jurisdiction above (pulled vs mapped vs missing) and are included in the downloadable full CSVs. A few jurisdictions publish no coordinates at all — their records are CSV-only. Coordinate precision varies by jurisdiction (some portals round coordinates). Categories differ between jurisdictions; the original classification is always preserved next to the standardized one.
